### 2. Jiji

Jiji is a Nigerian-origin marketplace operating across West Africa. It has large inventory, particularly in Nigeria and Ghana, but charges for premium placement.

**Platform:** Android + iOS + Web
**Best for:** Nigeria, Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire
**Downside:** Pay-to-boost model, foreign-owned

---

### 3. Jumia

Jumia is Africa's largest e-commerce platform, similar to Amazon. It sells new products from registered merchants — not a peer-to-peer marketplace.

**Platform:** Android + iOS + Web
**Best for:** Buying new goods, branded products
**Downside:** Not for individual sellers, no second-hand items

---

### 4. Facebook Marketplace

Facebook Marketplace is widely used across West Africa due to Facebook's massive user base in the region. However, it lacks structure and local support.

**Platform:** Android + iOS (within Facebook app)
**Best for:** Quick local sales
**Downside:** No dedicated app, safety issues, not Africa-focused
