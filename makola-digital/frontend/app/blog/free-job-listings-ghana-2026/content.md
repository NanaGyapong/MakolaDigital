### 2. Jobberman Ghana

Jobberman is one of Africa's largest job platforms. It has a large database of CVs and job listings. Premium packages can get expensive for small businesses.

**Best for:** Corporate jobs, large employers
**Downside:** Free tier has limited visibility

### 3. LinkedIn

LinkedIn is the global professional network. Great for formal sector jobs but less effective for blue-collar, trade, and SME jobs common in Ghana.

**Best for:** Corporate, tech, management roles
**Downside:** Not Ghana-specific, requires internet data

### 4. Ghana Jobs Web (GhanaJobsWeb)

A dedicated Ghanaian job board with many NGO, government, and private sector listings.

**Best for:** NGO and government jobs
**Downside:** Less active for SME and informal sector

### 5. Facebook Groups

Many Ghana-specific Facebook job groups exist (e.g. "Jobs in Ghana", "Accra Jobs"). Free but unstructured and hard to track applications.

**Best for:** Quick informal job posts
**Downside:** Hard to manage, lots of spam

## Tips for Job Seekers in Ghana 2026

### 1. Build a Strong WhatsApp Profile
Many Ghanaian employers contact candidates via WhatsApp. Make sure your profile picture is professional and your status is appropriate.

### 2. Polish Your CV
Keep it to 2 pages maximum. Include:
- Contact details (phone + email)
- A short professional summary
- Work experience (most recent first)
- Education and certifications
- References (2 minimum)

### 3. Use Multiple Platforms
Don't rely on one job site. Post your CV on Makola Digital, Jobberman, and LinkedIn simultaneously.

### 4. Network Actively
In Ghana, many jobs are filled through connections before they're even advertised. Join professional WhatsApp groups, attend industry events, and keep in touch with former colleagues.

### 5. Consider Freelancing
Many Ghanaians are earning good income through freelance work — graphic design, copywriting, social media management, and data entry can all be done remotely.

## Tips for Employers Hiring in Ghana 2026

### 1. Be Specific in Your Job Ad
Include exact requirements, salary range (even approximate), location, and how to apply. Vague ads get poor response rates.

### 2. Respond Quickly
Ghana's job market is competitive. Good candidates receive multiple offers. Respond within 24-48 hours.

### 3. Use WhatsApp for Screening
Initial screening via WhatsApp is common and efficient in Ghana. Ask candidates to send their CV to your WhatsApp number.

### 4. Post on Free Platforms First
Before paying for premium job boards, try free platforms like Makola Digital — many small businesses find great hires there at zero cost.

### 5. Be Clear About Salary
Listing a salary range attracts more serious applicants and saves time filtering unsuitable candidates.
