## How to Avoid Fake or Stolen Phones in Accra

🚨 **Red flags when buying phones in Accra:**

1. **IMEI check fails** — always dial *#06# to see the IMEI and verify it against the box
2. **No original box** — budget phones especially should come boxed
3. **Cracked software** — unofficial software/jailbreak may indicate stolen device
4. **"Factory fresh" at unbelievable price** — if it's too cheap, something is wrong
5. **Seller won't let you test** — always insist on 10 minutes of testing

**How to check an iPhone's legitimacy:**
- Go to Settings → General → About → check IMEI
- Visit Apple's coverage checker at checkcoverage.apple.com
- Ensure it's not iCloud locked (activation lock)
